How it works

Four steps, about three seconds

  1. 1

    You speak

    In English, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu or Malayalam, on the website or a phone call.

  2. 2

    It listens

    ElevenLabs turns your voice into text and detects your language.

  3. 3

    It searches

    The agent looks up the college's official sources live, using Google Search through Gemini, so it works for any college.

  4. 4

    It answers

    ElevenLabs speaks the answer in a natural voice and shows the sources.

Built with ElevenLabs Agents · Vapi · Gemini + Google Search · Vercel

Why I built this

Every student deserves an answer.

During admissions, college offices are flooded with calls. Phones ring unanswered, websites are confusing and mostly in English. A first-generation student, or a parent who speaks only Tamil, Hindi, Telugu or Malayalam, often can't get a simple answer: "What's the fee? Is there a hostel? When's the last date?"

Wrong or missing information costs real seats and real money. So I built a helpdesk that never sleeps, never gets tired of questions and speaks your language. It works for any college, on a website or a simple phone call.

No student should miss an opportunity just because nobody picked up the phone.
